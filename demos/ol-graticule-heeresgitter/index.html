Deutsches Heeresgitter

@zwaarcontrast/ol-graticule-heeresgitter

WWII Wehrmacht grids: Deutsches Heeresgitter (6° Gauß-Krüger on the Bessel 1841 ellipsoid, with Kennziffer-prefixed eastings) and the orange Heeresmeldenetz letter-cell overprint in both variants: planar (6 km Kleinquadrate, anchored to the DHG km lattice) and geographic (6′ × 4′ Kleintrapeze anchored to 0°40′N × 0°E). Both variants use the same alphabet without I.

Sources: Planheft Schweiz (OKH g 23/1, 16 March 1944), Capturing the Complex Histories of German WWII Captured Maps (Powell & Mühr, 2020), and Geodetic grids in authoritative maps: new findings about the origin of the UTM Grid (Buchroithner & Pfahlbusch, 2016), which gives the explicit spec for the geographic HMN variant.
